# Changelog

All notable changes to this project are documented in this file.

The format is based on [Keep a Changelog](https://keepachangelog.com/en/1.1.0/).

## Unreleased

### Added

- Shasha for Android phones and tablets, Android TV and Windows.
- Continue on another device, Continue here, Play on another device, and the phone as a remote for the TV.
- Device sync on your own Firebase database, with accounts that follow you, encrypted with your passphrase.
- Player: seeking that adds up with a side overlay, scrubbing with a frame preview, skip intro and credits, speed up to 5x, Ambient mode, Enhance video, auto captions with translation, the original audio language first, Picture-in-Picture and the in-app mini player.
- Play next, Add to queue, Loop and Autoplay.
- One audio player for every audio source, with lyrics and live subtitles, Up next and a sleep timer.
- YouTube with your own Home, Watch later, Like, Subscribe, comments, History and a Shorts player.
- YouTube Music and SoundCloud.
- Search filters by kind, and links from YouTube, YouTube Music, Dailymotion and SoundCloud open in the app.
- IPTV with Xtream Codes and M3U accounts.
- Extension discovery from the community list.
- Profiles with PIN, per-device defaults and per-profile language, accent and extensions.
- Themes: Default, Material You and Glass, all coloured by your accent, with a launcher icon that follows them.
- English and Arabic with right-to-left layouts; Inter and Noto Sans Arabic.
